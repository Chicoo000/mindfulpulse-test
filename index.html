MindfulPulse

A digital “Care Sister” for newly arrived foreign domestic workers in Singapore. Thank you for helping us test it.

Before you start

  • This is a student project. You are chatting with a computer helper, not a real person or a doctor.
  • Do not share your real name, address, phone number, passport number or employer’s details.
  • Chats are stored by the service for testing. They are not shared with any employer.
  • Write in any language: English, Tagalog, Bahasa Indonesia, Burmese, Tamil, Hindi and more.

Please test everyday situations only

  • Try: feeling tired or homesick, too much work, asking for a rest day, questions about rest days or salary.
  • Please do not role-play abuse or self-harm.

Need to talk to a real person? CDE Helpline 1800 225 5233 (24 hours, free) · In immediate danger: Police 999 · Samaritans of Singapore 1767 (24 hours).

A prototype built in Google CX Agent Studio for a university course. Not an official support service.
